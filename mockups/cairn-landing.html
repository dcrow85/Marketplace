Meet Anko

The whole floor, in plain English.

Anko can put the right cards at the top, find copies across every table, explain a trade, and ask for better evidence. His intelligence is an asset—not a hidden authority.

Anko can recommend and prepare actions. He cannot spend, accept, or hand off your cards unless you give that authority explicitly.

Trust, without theater

Clear about what Cairn knows.

A scan can add clarity. It cannot prove a physical card is authentic. Cairn keeps those boundaries visible instead of collapsing every kind of trust into one badge.

Judged

Authenticity & condition

People, agents, or arbiters still interpret off-chain physical facts. Cairn does not turn an image into certainty.

Settle with intent

Checkout when it's a buy. A conversation when it isn't.

Paying a seller's posted ask should feel like checkout. Changing price, cards, or evidence turns it into an offer. Cairn keeps that handoff unmistakable.
