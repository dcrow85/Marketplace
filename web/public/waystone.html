The waystone at the edge of the village remembers. When a trade truly settles, the stone keeps the record — green means recorded. When a seller says a card is near-mint, the stone keeps that too, as their claim, plainly marked. The village never pretends to verify authenticity or condition; it records claims, locks terms, and keeps honest memory.
