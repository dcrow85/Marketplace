# Waystone concept art — INTERNAL ONLY

Everything in this directory is AI-generated concept/mood material
(scripts/waystone_art.py, DeepInfra). Per Waystone_World_v0.1:

- These images are composition studies and artist-brief material.
- They are NEVER the shipped public face of Waystone.
- The final village art is commissioned from a human artist, who reinterprets
  these studies (and the live coded stage) in a hand-drawn way.
- Each .png has a .json sidecar recording model, prompt, seed, date.
