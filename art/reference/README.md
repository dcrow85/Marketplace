# DERIVATIVE — internal reference only, never publish

These two images were generated by style-transferring the work of a specific
living artist, **Deth P. Sun** (dethpsun.com), from a painting Crowley owns.
They are not "inspired by" — they are too close, and Crowley flagged it.

They are kept here ONLY as a record of what the style test proved (flat colour
as light reads better than glow; ink silhouettes are native; scale by nature
works), and they must never be published, deployed, or used as a brief for
generation.

His vocabulary — the black cat protagonist, the salmon-coral accents, the
chevron-hatched triangular peaks, the scalloped clouds, the blue-grey/black/coral
triad — is HIS. Waystone's creature must not be a small black cat.

The right path is to commission him. The fallback is to diverge into a
different tradition entirely (white-line wood engraving; mokuhanga), not to
make a near-copy with the numbers filed off.
